IT Support in Ho Chi Minh City for Foreign Companies

You run an office in Vietnam, your head office is overseas, and nobody locally owns IT. ITSS is a bilingual (English / Vietnamese) IT team in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi that keeps your office network, accounts and devices running.

Book a free 30-minute office IT review

What we handle for you

IT helpdesk & support

Remote and on-site help for PCs, email, accounts and printers, with one point of contact in your language.

Office network setup

Firewall, switching, Wi-Fi and VPN for a new office or a relocation, with network diagram and handover documents.

Email & workspace

Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace administration, mailbox migration, mail filtering, SPF / DKIM / DMARC.

Hardware quotes

Quotes for any brand and type of IT equipment, matched to your budget and delivered and installed locally.

Phone & access systems

PBX, virtual numbers forwarded to Vietnam and card-access setup.

Systems administration

Monitoring, user and permission management and config backup, reported monthly.

Why foreign-owned offices choose a local IT partner

Overseas IT teams often cannot visit, do not know local ISPs and suppliers, and work in a different time zone. We act as your local hands and eyes: we coordinate with your head-office IT, document everything in English, and answer in 1 business day. Our lead has 6+ years of IT infrastructure and operations experience and holds ITIL Foundation v5 and Certified ScrumMaster certifications.

How it works

  1. Free 30-minute review of your office IT and network (video call or on site).
  2. Written scope and quote within 1 business day. Plans (Basic / Standard / Premium) and projects are quoted per need.
  3. Deploy and hand over, then optional monthly support.

FAQ

Can you support offices owned by Swiss, European, Korean, Japanese or Singaporean companies?

Yes. We work in English and Vietnamese and coordinate with your overseas IT provider.

Can we start with a single project?

Yes. A network setup or a hardware quote can be a one-off; monthly support is optional.

Which cities?

Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi on site; remote support elsewhere in Vietnam.
